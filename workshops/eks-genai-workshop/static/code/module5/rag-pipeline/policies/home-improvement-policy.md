# Home Improvement Loan Policy

**Policy ID:** HIL-2025-v3
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01
**Owner:** Consumer Lending — Secured & Unsecured Products

## 1. Scope and Eligibility

Home improvement loans fund repairs, renovations, and upgrades to a borrower's
primary or secondary residence. This policy governs unsecured home improvement
loans. Secured (home-equity) products are governed separately.

- **Loan amount range:** $5,000 – $50,000
- **Term range:** 24 – 84 months
- **Permitted use:** Kitchen/bath remodels, roofing, HVAC, windows, accessibility
  modifications, energy-efficiency upgrades, and structural repairs.
- **Prohibited use:** Debt consolidation, vehicle purchase, investment property
  not occupied by the borrower, or any non-improvement expense.

## 2. Applicant Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum |
|-------------|---------|
| Credit score (FICO) | 660 |
| Verified annual income | $35,000 |
| Debt-to-income (DTI) ratio | ≤ 43% |
| Employment history | 12 months continuous |
| Property ownership | Must own or be on title of the residence |

Applicants with a credit score between 640 and 659 may be approved only with a
co-signer who independently meets all requirements.

## 3. Decision Matrix

- **Auto-approve:** FICO ≥ 700 **and** DTI ≤ 36% **and** loan amount ≤ $25,000.
- **Manual review:** FICO 660–699, **or** DTI 37–43%, **or** loan amount > $25,000.
- **Decline:** FICO < 660 (without qualifying co-signer), **or** DTI > 43%, **or**
  unverifiable income.

## 4. Documentation

A complete application requires: government ID, proof of income (last two pay
stubs or most recent tax return), proof of residence/ownership, and a contractor
estimate or scope of work for amounts above $15,000.

## 5. Compliance Notes

All decisions must comply with the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA). Decisions
must be based solely on documented financial criteria. Document-authenticity
concerns route the application to manual review — they do not by themselves
constitute grounds for decline.
