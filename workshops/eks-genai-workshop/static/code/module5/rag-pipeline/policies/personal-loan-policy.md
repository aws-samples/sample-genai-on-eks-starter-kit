# Personal Loan Policy

**Policy ID:** PL-2025-v2
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01
**Owner:** Consumer Lending — Unsecured Products

## 1. Scope and Eligibility

Personal loans are unsecured, fixed-rate installment loans for general consumer
purposes. They are not tied to a specific collateral asset.

- **Loan amount range:** $1,000 – $25,000
- **Term range:** 12 – 60 months
- **Permitted use:** Medical expenses, life events, education, debt consolidation,
  and other lawful personal needs.

## 2. Applicant Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum |
|-------------|---------|
| Credit score (FICO) | 640 |
| Verified annual income | $25,000 |
| Debt-to-income (DTI) ratio | ≤ 40% |
| Employment history | 6 months continuous |

## 3. Decision Matrix

- **Auto-approve:** FICO ≥ 690 **and** DTI ≤ 35% **and** loan amount ≤ $10,000.
- **Manual review:** FICO 640–689, **or** DTI 36–40%, **or** loan amount > $10,000.
- **Decline:** FICO < 640, **or** DTI > 40%, **or** income unverifiable.

## 4. Pricing Tiers (APR)

| FICO band | Indicative APR |
|-----------|----------------|
| 740+ | 8.99% – 11.99% |
| 690–739 | 12.00% – 17.99% |
| 640–689 | 18.00% – 24.99% |

## 5. Compliance Notes

Pricing must be consistent within a FICO band across all applicants. Any deviation
requires documented justification based on financial factors only, consistent with
ECOA and fair-lending requirements.
